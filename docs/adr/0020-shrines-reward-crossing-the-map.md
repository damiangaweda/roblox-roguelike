# Shrines reward crossing the map

**Status:** accepted on 2026-10-28.

After the first playtest of stage 3 the user found no reason to cross the map before the compass points at the chest: the horde comes to the hero wherever they stand. We add shrines: a few places scattered with the obstacles, each of one of four kinds (a boon, a pact, a trial, a spring), seen from afar by a beam of light, activated by a hold that a hit interrupts, each a decision about the build or a risk taken under the horde's pressure. We chose this over items dropped by elites (which come to the hero and ask nothing) and over scattered experience or gold (which the horde already pays) because a shrine is a place: it pulls the hero across the map, makes the obstacles matter and gives a run its own geography.

## Considered options

- **Elites drop in-run relics:** rewards without moving; parked earlier as relics.
- **Crystals and food scattered at the start:** the pull fades as the horde grows and the hero outpaces the pickups.
- **Events on a clock (a caravan, a siege) at a marked place:** a stronger pull, but a timer the player cannot choose; kept for later.

## Consequences

A shrine's kind is data and its pools are configuration, so maps may later get their own shrines. The trial reuses the closing ring and the offer, and the great offer is the one card draw with bigger ranks, so no second card system exists. The compass never points at a shrine: finding them is the point.
